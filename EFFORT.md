## Entering the framework

A language supplies its syntax, its semantics and a translation into the core. All sixteen logics on each face, their soundness and completeness, and the generic proof rules then come with it. The files below also hold each language's worked material (rule lemmas, small examples), so they over-count the entry cost.

## Comparison

A hand-written logic covers one corner, at about 155 lines on average here; sixteen corners by hand would be about 2480 lines per language. Entering the framework costs 78 to 470 lines per language, once, for all sixteen corners.
